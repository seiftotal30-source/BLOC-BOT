DRV8833 motor circuit

One DRV8833 board drives both TT motors from the 2S battery. The ESP32 sends PWM straight to the four IN pins, and STBY is tied to 3.3 V so the driver stays awake. It needs no 5 V supply of its own.

battery / 5 V positive ground (all tied together) 3.3 V to STBY optional for the bench test PWM signals
STBY must be HIGH. The chip pulls it low inside. If STBY is left unconnected, nothing moves.
Check the chip marking. This wiring is for a DRV8833. If the black chip says TB6612, the NC pins are PWMA, PWMB and VCC, and the wiring changes.
No capacitor yet? Fine for the bench test with the wheels in the air. The board already has a 10 µF capacitor on VM. Add the 470 µF and the 0.1 µF motor capacitors before floor tests.
Still on the DevKit V1? GPIO 15 is a boot strapping pin there. Move AIN1 to another free pin.

Keep duty at or below about 70 %. Full duty puts up to 8.4 V on motors rated 3–6 V. If a motor spins the wrong way, swap its two wires at AO or BO.
