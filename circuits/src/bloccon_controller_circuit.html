Bloc-Con controller circuit

One 18650 cell feeds a TP4056 charger and an MT3608 boost set to 5 V, which powers the ESP32. The OLED and joystick run from the ESP32's 3.3 V pin. The four buttons pull their GPIO to GND (internal pull-ups). Pins match Bloc-Con/src/main.cpp.

battery / 5 V 3.3 V from the ESP32 ground (all tied together) net tag: wire to the ESP32 pin of that name
Set the MT3608 to 5.0 V first. Power it from the cell with nothing on the output, turn the trimmer until a multimeter reads 5.0 V, then connect the ESP32. Out of the box it can put out far more and kill the board.
Joystick on 3.3 V, not 5 V. The ESP32 ADC tops out at 3.3 V. The centre should read near 1800–2048; your last calibration (X 990, Y 616) says to check this wiring.
HC-SR04 ECHO is 5 V. GPIO 18 is a 3.3 V pin. Put a divider on ECHO (1 kΩ in series, 2 kΩ to GND) once you have resistors, or leave the sensor off the controller until then.
Switch goes after the TP4056, on OUT+, so the cell still charges over USB-C with the controller off.
Switch off before plugging USB into the ESP32 to upload, so the boost and the USB 5 V do not fight on the 5V pin.

No resistors needed: the firmware uses INPUT_PULLUP, so a pressed button reads LOW. On 4-leg tactile switches, use two diagonal legs. The 2.42" SSD1309 (I²C version) uses the same four OLED wires, with USE_SSD1309 1.
